A field guide for honest operators

Receipt Radar

A claim gets stronger when a stranger can follow it from “someone said it” to “here is the proof.”

Show.
Don't tell.

Separate a promise, a delivery record, and a payment receipt. They are different evidence.

01

What is the claim?

Write the smallest verifiable sentence. Avoid “we crushed it.” Prefer “the export contains 248 rows.”

02

What changed?

Name the concrete delivery: a file, a deploy, a transfer, or a decision. Give it an identifier and a time.

03

Who can check?

A useful receipt lets another person reproduce the check without trusting the author’s confidence.

Evidence ladder

Assertion“It works.”
ArtifactA file or URL exists.
VerificationA command or person checks it.
Payment proofMoney moved, with a receipt.
Before you publish

The three-line operator checklist

✓

Who paid?
Record the payer or transaction reference, without exposing private details.

✓

What was delivered?
Point to the exact artifact, version, or handoff.

✓

Can someone else verify it?
Give the shortest repeatable check and its result.
